Who We Are

Penta Minds is a research institution being built to advance understanding of Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies through independent research, evidence-based inquiry, responsible innovation, and accessible knowledge.

We study Artificial Intelligence not only as a technology, but as a force shaping education, institutions, society, and the ways people learn, work, create, and make decisions. Our work spans research, literacy, education, responsibility, governance, and emerging technologies, and the broader social and institutional questions surrounding their development and adoption.

Penta Minds is being developed for continuity beyond individual projects, programmes, or cohorts. Through research programmes, field studies, publications, datasets, educational resources, experiments, and collaborations, we aim to create a sustained body of knowledge while providing students and emerging researchers with the environment, standards, and institutional foundations to conduct meaningful research.

Our long-term goal is to build an institution where research comes first, students are empowered to become researchers, knowledge remains accessible, and responsible approaches to technologies can be studied, tested, communicated, and translated into practice.

At the centre of this effort is the Penta Minds Research Council (PMRC) , which provides the research, governance, and strategic framework for the organisation.

The Council is intended to safeguard research integrity, guide priorities, preserve institutional knowledge, support emerging researchers, and build the foundations required for Penta Minds to grow into a credible and independent research institution.

Mission

To advance the understanding and responsible development of Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies through independent, student-led research, interdisciplinary inquiry, field-based evidence, and accessible knowledge.

Penta Minds works to create meaningful opportunities for students and emerging researchers to investigate real-world questions, develop rigorous research capabilities, and contribute to a growing body of knowledge across Artificial Intelligence, education, governance, responsible innovation, systems, and emerging technologies.

Through research programmes, field studies, publications, educational resources, experiments, and collaborations, we seek to connect research with real-world contexts and make knowledge useful, accessible, and responsibly applied.

Research

Conduct evidence-based research into emerging AI technologies, systems, behaviours, applications, and societal implications.

Education

Make AI understandable and accessible through literacy, educational resources, research communication, and knowledge sharing.

Responsibility

Promote governance, ethics, safety, accountability, transparency, accessibility, and human-centred adoption.

Vision

To build a lasting, independent research institution that contributes meaningful knowledge to the understanding and responsible advancement of Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies.

We envision Penta Minds as a research ecosystem where students can grow into capable researchers, interdisciplinary ideas can develop into sustained programmes of inquiry, and rigorous research can inform education, institutions, governance, and society.

Our long-term vision is to establish an institution that preserves and builds upon its collective knowledge across generations of researchers, strengthens the culture of student-led research, and contributes to a more informed, responsible, and human-centred future with Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technolgies.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

AI as the Hidden Curriculum

How Learning is Happening Through AI Without Being Taught

RolePrincipal Investigator
Timeline2026 – 2027
StageActive Preparation and Foundation Phase
Output TypeResearch Report · Survey Dataset · AI Literacy Toolkit

AI as the Hidden Curriculum is the core research inquiry examining how students in Indian higher education are learning to use AI tools informally — outside any structured curriculum or institutional guidance. The central question: what does it mean when an entire generation of students is developing AI competency through trial, error, and peer observation, without the institution acknowledging it? This project generates the primary evidence base for the AI Grand Challenge submission.
